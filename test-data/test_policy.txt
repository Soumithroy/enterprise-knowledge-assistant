Enterprise Vacation Policy

Employees receive 20 vacation days per calendar year.

Vacation requests should be submitted through the employee portal.

Requests longer than five consecutive business days require manager approval.

Unused vacation days may be carried forward according to company policy.

Employees should submit vacation requests at least five business days before the requested start date.

Managers are responsible for reviewing vacation requests and ensuring adequate team coverage.

Employees should coordinate planned vacation periods with their immediate team members.

Emergency leave should be communicated to the manager as soon as reasonably possible.

The company encourages employees to use their vacation allowance throughout the year.

Vacation policies may vary depending on employment classification and applicable company regulations.

Employees should contact Human Resources if they have questions about their available vacation balance.

The employee portal provides the current available vacation balance for each employee.

Managers should respond to vacation requests within three business days.

Long periods of leave may require additional approval from Human Resources.
